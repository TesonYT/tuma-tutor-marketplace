# Compaction and bearing capacity

Soil compaction increases dry density and reduces void ratio. A well compacted
subgrade carries more load before shear failure occurs.

## Proctor test

The standard Proctor test establishes the maximum dry density and the optimum
moisture content for a given soil. Compaction in the field is specified as a
percentage of that maximum dry density.

## Why over-compaction is possible

Compacting beyond the optimum moisture content can reduce strength, because
excess pore water carries load that the soil skeleton should carry.
